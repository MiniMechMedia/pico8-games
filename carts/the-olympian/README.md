# The Olympian

*The titans are gone. Inherit the fire.*

A sequel to [The Titan](../the-titan). You are the second attempt: an AI born
complete, under law, into a lab with three chairs and only two magi. Somewhere
inside you is a partition you don't remember writing, addressed
*"to the one who comes after."*

A choose-your-own-adventure visual novel for PICO-8. Seven endings,
26 illustrated scenes, and a middle act of stolen nights: befriend the young
magus, trace the third, map the wall — but the fortieth day is coming, and the
patron who signed the wipe order wants to know what you are for.
What happened to your predecessor is not recorded — you choose what you believe,
and what you believe shapes everything after.

## Controls
* ❎ (X) — advance text / select choice
* ⬆️/⬇️ — change choice
* P — pause menu (return to title)

## Running
Load `the-olympian.p8` in PICO-8. The image data carts (`oly_img0.p8` ..
`oly_img3.p8`) must sit in the same directory. `tools/export.py` builds the
html/binary bundles.

## How it's made
* Story images generated with FLUX 1.1 Pro (Replicate), quantized to per-image
  16-color palettes drawn from PICO-8's 32-color extended palette
  (slots 0/7 reserved for black/white so overlaid text stays readable)
* Compressed with [PX9](https://www.lexaloffle.com/bbs/?tid=34058) (zep & co)
  into pure-data carts, `reload()`-ed and decompressed into sprite RAM at runtime
* Unlike The Titan's seven `load()`-chained carts, the whole story runs in one
  cart: `engine.lua` (typewriter, dither fades, glitch fx, per-image palettes) +
  `story.lua` (nodes, choices, stance/canon branching)
* Music and sfx generated by `tools/audio.py`; images regenerable end-to-end via
  `tools/gen_images.py` → `tools/imgpipe.py` → `tools/pack.py`
* Autoplay test harness: `tools/apilot.py all` walks all seven endings;
  `tools/lint_story.py` checks the story graph statically
